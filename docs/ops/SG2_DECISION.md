## Why option 3

1. **Market is PA-only.** An empty pack that is *labelled* empty is safer than one person's readings presented as governed answers to every tenant.
2. **Solo operating reality.** `approve_regulatory_rule_version` needs a second identity; a second account you control satisfies the check without producing a second *reader*. Counting that as review would be dishonest in a survey or incident review.
3. **Option 3 is free and reversible.** Options 1 and 2 are not. BACKLOG already said: decide before authoring, not after.
4. **Ohio template stays mechanism-only.** `oh.rcf.3701-16.personnel` demonstrates install/submit/shadow/activate. It must never be implied to cover Pennsylvania.
